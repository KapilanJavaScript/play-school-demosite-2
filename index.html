FAQ

Questions parents ask us

What is the right age to start playschool?
Most children are ready for Play Group between 1.5 and 2.5 years, and infant care from 8 months. Brain development peaks in the first six years, so a gentle, play-based start builds skills that last a lifetime.
Is the campus safe? How is it monitored?
Yes — we run 24×7 CCTV coverage, gated entry, child-proofed classrooms and trained housekeeping staff. Safety is a promise, not a feature.
What does my child actually learn in a day?
Every day balances language, numbers, music, art, motor skills and social play — a structured yet playful routine that builds confidence and school-readiness.
Do you offer full-day daycare for working parents?
Yes. Daycare runs from 8 AM to 6:30 PM with meals, structured activities and rest — with updates to parents through the day.
How do I book a visit or secure admission?
Simply use the enrolment form below, or WhatsApp us. We'll schedule a tour and walk you through fees and the admission process.
